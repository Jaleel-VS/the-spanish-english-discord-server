## Open the invite

The invite is [discord.gg/spanish-english](https://discord.gg/spanish-english). It works in the Discord app on any device and straight in a browser. If you don't have a Discord account yet, Discord will ask you to make one on the way in: a username, an email address and a password.

## Accept the rules

The first thing you see is the rules channel. Read it; it is short. You accept the rules by completing Discord's membership screening, and until you do you can read but not write.

## Say what you speak

Discord then asks a few onboarding questions: which language you are learning, roughly what level you are at, and which one you speak natively. Your answers give you roles. The roles are how other members know whether to write to you in Spanish or English, and how corrections find the right people.

You can change your answers later from **Channels & Roles** at the top of the channel list.

## The ten-minute wait

New members can't post for the first ten minutes. This is a Discord setting the server uses (the "high" verification level), and it keeps spam accounts out. Your account also needs a verified email address and must be at least five minutes old. If you still can't post after ten minutes, check that your email is verified in Discord's settings.

## Your first message

Write in the language you are learning, in one of the practice channels. Someone who speaks it will answer, usually within minutes. If you'd like your mistakes pointed out, say so, or add a pencil to your name with the `;pencil` command (see [the Rai bot](/bots/rai)).
