Rai is the bot the moderators rely on. It logs joins, leaves, edits and deletions, screens new accounts, handles mutes and bans with a duration, and keeps the questions queue so a hard question doesn't get swept away in the channel.

Members use it too: hardcore mode, your language stats, the leaderboards and questions. The commands members use most are below.
